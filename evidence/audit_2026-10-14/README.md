# Direct source proof

The 41 recovered V4 crops reproduce their original recorded SHA-256 values. Two crops share image bytes. Full-page context images are newly rendered, not claimed to be byte-identical to historical full-page captures. Original PDF page numbering and printed numbering are separate. The scanned Xbox manual was visually reviewed without OCR.

The [proof manifest](proof_manifest.csv) records source hashes and actual files. [Validation](validation.json) reports structural checks, not total canon certainty.
