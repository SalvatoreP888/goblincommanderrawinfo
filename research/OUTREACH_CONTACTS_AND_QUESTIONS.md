## First round: resolve story, find archives, unblock engineering

### 1. Christopher Zirpoli — writer / goblin voice

**Route:** [official contact form](https://www.chriszirpoli.com/contact). Form page rechecked. No direct email guessed. [His own résumé](https://www.chriszirpoli.com/resume) is also a lead for Jaleco-era websites and cinematic work.

Ask initially:
1. Did a story bible, full cinematic/dialogue script, clan dossier or world timeline exist, and does an identifiable dated copy survive?
2. What did Fraziel originally intend? Prima p. 99 calls Naxus's actions a perversion, while p. 108 reports Naxus saying soul bombs were always the machine's purpose. Is that contradiction intentional, a character's unreliable claim, or a guide/script revision difference?
3. Was anything written about other humans, the builders of the ancient crypts/Moongates, the Moonstone's origin, or Naxus's fate after defeat?

Follow-up only after interest: who authored the English/French official-site clan profiles; do backups survive; does his résumé's AMC/Return of the King trailer reference concern this particular game? Do not assume that attribution in the question.

### 2. Ron / Ronald Millar — lead designer

**Route:** **ronaldmillar@gmail.com**, publicly listed on [his own résumé](https://ronmillar.com/resume/), rechecked. This is the strongest direct-email route among the story/design leads.

Ask:
1. Which parts of Ogriss's prehistory were designed but never explained in the retail game? Were humans, moons or ancient cultures defined beyond the manual?
2. Was the five-clan counter-cycle intended as story/worldbuilding, or purely game balance? Did the relationship between Fraziel's unique Moonstone, purchased clan Moonstones and the five moons have a written explanation?

Do not lead with 'which eight missions were cut': early 25-level and later 17-campaign/29-total counts are not equivalent categories.

## Shared questions for any helpful reply

Establish whether the answer is recollection, an unused draft, a released-game fact or an identifiable dated document. Ask which platform/build/date it refers to. Ask whether the exact words may be quoted and whether an attachment may be reproduced publicly; a reply alone does not grant either permission. Ask for a preferred credit and one relevant introduction. Keep originals privately, then publish only approved quotations/artifacts with their provenance.
