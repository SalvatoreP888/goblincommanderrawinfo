## Named individuals and moons

The personally named figures are **Fraziel, Grommel, Grax, Faine, Syst, and Naxus**. **The Wise One**, also called the Old Man, is an additional title-based character whose personal name is not supplied. The moons **Froxx, Heelinx, Trist, Phoust, and Farthis** are celestial objects, not confirmed gods or additional people. See the [character reference](CHARACTERS.md).

## Important connections

The five clans form the counter cycle **Stonekrusher → Nighthorde → Hellfire → Plaguespitter → Stormbringer → Stonekrusher**. No source says Fraziel intentionally designed this balance. [Prima clan entries](../prima/Goblin_Commander_Prima_2004_PAGE_INDEXED.md#pdf-page-016).

Faine's weather machinery, Syst's Defiler Tree, Nighthorde's soul theft, and the final machine's soul-severing weapons make the world more interconnected than a simple five-faction war. They are nevertheless **distinct documented mechanisms**, not proof that every device shares one ancient origin. [Prima p. 78](../evidence/audit_2026-10-14/prima/printed_p078_full_context.png) · [p. 82](../evidence/audit_2026-10-14/prima/printed_p082_full_context.png) · [p. 108](../evidence/audit_2026-10-14/prima/printed_p108_full_context.png).

Ordinary purchasable **Nighthorde Moonstones are black**, while Farthis and the final Nighthorde-associated Moongate are purple. Their relationship to Fraziel's creation artifact is unknown. [Prima p. 11](../evidence/audit_2026-10-14/prima/printed_p011_full_context.png) · [manual pp. 22–23](../evidence/audit_2026-10-14/manual_printed_22_23.png).

The [September 26, 2003 Xbox prototype](https://hiddenpalace.org/Goblin_Commander:_Unleash_the_Horde_(Sep_26,_2003_prototype)) has preservation records, but an authenticated unused script or complete retail comparison has not been established. [Chris Millar's 2018 account](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/reversing-the-sunk-cost-fallacy-devs-recount-what-shouldn-t-have-been-cut) supports working online multiplayer being cut; the [official-site announcement](https://www.nintendoworldreport.com/pr/8827/goblin-commander-website-open) supports planned clan spotlights, not the recovery of every promised page.

## Proof actually preserved

The original Prima PDF and original Xbox manual are stored in this repository. The audit reproduced **41 original passage captures with matching hashes** and indexed **85 proof-image records**. Two passage captures share image bytes. Supporting passages were rechecked for **52 claim entries**; this is not a claim that all 133 entries or every retail mechanic were freshly verified.

Use the [proof manifest](../evidence/audit_2026-10-14/proof_manifest.csv), [validation results](../evidence/audit_2026-10-14/validation.json), and [source-proof overview](../evidence/audit_2026-10-14/README.md). Older statements that these PNGs or the manual were not committed are superseded.

The remaining limits are recorded in the [project audit](PROJECT_AUDIT_2026-10-14.md): no complete raw export of every project branch, no fresh audit of the current local browser-port code and wave19 traces, and incomplete recovery of original maps, website assets, and video/dialogue references.
