## Seven named individuals/roles

Fraziel, Grommel, Grax, Faine, Syst, Naxus, and **the Wise One** (also titled Old Man; personal name not supplied). The moons **Froxx, Heelinx, Trist, Phoust and Farthis** are named celestial objects, not confirmed deities or individual characters. [Original manual](https://www.gamesdatabase.org/Media/SYSTEM/Microsoft_Xbox//Manual/formated/Goblin_Commander_-_Unleash_The_Horde_-_2003_-_Jaleco.pdf).

## Major discoveries already in the V4 baseline

- The five clans form a counter cycle **Stonekrusher → Nighthorde → Hellfire → Plaguespitter → Stormbringer → Stonekrusher**; no source says Fraziel intentionally designed this cycle. [Prima pp. 15, 19, 23, 28, 33](../prima/Goblin_Commander_Prima_2004_PAGE_INDEXED.md#pdf-page-016).
- Faine has weather-control technology; Syst's Defiler Tree disables Soul Fountains; Nighthorde steals souls; the final machine weaponizes soul extraction at world scale. Those are distinct mechanisms, not proven to share a common origin. [Prima p. 78](../prima/Goblin_Commander_Prima_2004_PAGE_INDEXED.md#pdf-page-079) · [p. 82](../prima/Goblin_Commander_Prima_2004_PAGE_INDEXED.md#pdf-page-083) · [p. 108](../prima/Goblin_Commander_Prima_2004_PAGE_INDEXED.md#pdf-page-109).
- Ordinary purchasable **Nighthorde Moonstones are black**, while the final Moongate is purple. Their connection to Fraziel's unique creation artifact is unknown. [Prima p. 11](../prima/Goblin_Commander_Prima_2004_PAGE_INDEXED.md#pdf-page-012) · [p. 107](../prima/Goblin_Commander_Prima_2004_PAGE_INDEXED.md#pdf-page-108).
- A dated [September 26, 2003 Xbox prototype](https://hiddenpalace.org/Goblin_Commander:_Unleash_the_Horde_(Sep_26,_2003_prototype)) has preservation records, but a confirmed unused story script is **not** established.
- [Chris Millar's 2018 account](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/reversing-the-sunk-cost-fallacy-devs-recount-what-shouldn-t-have-been-cut) confirms working online multiplayer was cut; the [original official website announcement](https://www.nintendoworldreport.com/pr/8827/goblin-commander-website-open) confirms planned monthly clan spotlights but does not prove the Nighthorde profile was ever published.
