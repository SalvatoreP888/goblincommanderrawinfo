# Archive leads, world maps and exact mission index

14 October 2026. This file preserves leads from the project without turning a mentioned URL, visual reconstruction or unsupported sequel listing into canon.

## World-map fidelity

The user requested an updated, clean UHD map that remains 1:1 to the original canon. That requires an authenticated original map asset or source-page capture. A new attractive illustration cannot establish geography. Generated project visuals are design drafts, not historical evidence.

The original manual's Chronicle (printed pp. 22–23; viewer page 13 of the 19-page Xbox PDF) describes the clans' labor roles and broad geography: mines; Grax's eastern forests; Faine's southern mountains; Syst's western marshes; Naxus's ancient battlefields and search for lost wisdom. This is not a complete coastline, scale, projection or continent map. The guide's mission maps and multiplayer maps are local level layouts; they must not be merged into a planetary map merely because the campaign proceeds in that order.

For a genuine 1:1 reconstruction preserve source URL/file/hash, platform and publication revision, full original frame/page, dimensions, all labels and landmark positions. Separate global/campaign map, local mission map, multiplayer map, concept map and generated derivative. Record every redrawn feature against a source location. Where the map is absent, leave the geographic relation unknown rather than inventing kingdoms, capitals or coastlines.

**Open visual-source gap:** no authenticated master world-map asset or complete map-source catalog was recovered in this audit. Request it from the art/production contacts and website/press-kit archives. The user-requested generated map is not marked completed by this research audit.

## Other inherited leads and evidence limits

- [Original official website announcement](https://www.nintendoworldreport.com/pr/8827/goblin-commander-website-open): announced concept art, diary material, a first Stonekrusher profile and planned monthly profiles, plus a visual overhaul. Does not prove all five profiles were published or preserved.
- [Publisher material / June 2003 developer diary mirror](https://www.ladydragon.com/a-2goblin.html): period story and design context. Preserve the separate dated sections; early marketing is not always retail behavior.
- [Historical TeamXbox Russett interview destination](https://www.teamxbox.com/article.php?id=690&t=i), corroborating [Shacknews link](https://www.shacknews.com/article/29217/late-night-consoling): the destination must be recovered before quoting its interview contents.
- [Official Xbox magazine archive listing](https://archive.org/details/Xbox_RO_21): locate actual interview pages, language, issue date and full context.
- [2018 Chris Millar interview](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/reversing-the-sunk-cost-fallacy-devs-recount-what-shouldn-t-have-been-cut): firsthand testimony that working online play was removed. It is development history, not a playable retail feature.
- [September 26, 2003 Xbox prototype catalog](https://hiddenpalace.org/Goblin_Commander:_Unleash_the_Horde_(Sep_26,_2003_prototype)): damaged source preservation record. A prototype's existence does not establish recovered unused scenes, scripts or a completed comparison to retail.
- [Jaleco FY2004 corporate filing](https://www.hkexnews.hk/listedco/listconews/sehk/20050225/ltn20050225032.pdf): preserve the original fiscal/report date and platform/region context. A game-segment loss is not a Goblin Commander-specific loss.
- [OGDB title entry](https://ogdb.eu/index.php?section=title&titleid=3448): a purported sequel listing is a lead, not a produced sequel. Fanfiction such as 'Rise of the Horde' is not canon.
- A database GBA entry is not proof that a GBA port shipped or that a prototype existed.
- Fraziel's claimed eye/blood colors require actual cinematic frames; appearance claims from fan pages do not override the manual's identification as human.
- Full Fandom/Wayback inventories, original website backups and video transcripts remain incomplete. Mirrors and copied wiki summaries are not independent corroboration.

## Unresolved lore questions and the evidence that would settle them

**Fraziel:** birthplace, teachers, relatives, source of ancient knowledge, origin of the Moonstone and original machine purpose. Need dated script/bible or attributable creator explanation; do not infer a hidden human empire.

**Ancient Ogriss:** who made the crypts, battlefields and Moongates, how old they are, and where other humans lived. The presence of ruins establishes an older history, not its population or chronology.

**Moons and souls:** whether Fraziel's artifact and purchased Moonstones are related; whether the five moons are merely named celestial objects or were written with other significance; what the netherworld is beyond the Soul Vortex description. Named moons are not evidence of named gods.

**Naxus and ending:** intended fate after defeat, original versus revised final scenes, and whether any sequel material progressed beyond ideas. Separate the character's claims from omniscient narration and planned work from shipped fiction.

**Maps and cut content:** original geographic source, before/after art, authentic site profiles, alternate dialogue and complete prototype comparison. Every answer must state source type and revision. See the outreach packets for the appropriate person and precise question.
