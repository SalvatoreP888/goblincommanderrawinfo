# Named Goblin Commander characters, titles, and moons
Updated 2026-10-14 · Canon or licensed-guide identifications; no fan-fiction people promoted to canon.

## Six personal names plus one title-based NPC

| Character | Identification | Source and fate |
|---|---|---|
| **Fraziel** | Human wizard, created the five races and their commanders; master behind Great Machine | [Prima p. 4](../prima/Goblin_Commander_Prima_2004_PAGE_INDEXED.md#pdf-page-005), [p. 99](../prima/Goblin_Commander_Prima_2004_PAGE_INDEXED.md#pdf-page-100): murdered by Naxus |
| **Grommel** | Stonekrusher leader, eldest commander; strives to unify goblins | [Prima p. 4](../prima/Goblin_Commander_Prima_2004_PAGE_INDEXED.md#pdf-page-005), [p. 112](../prima/Goblin_Commander_Prima_2004_PAGE_INDEXED.md#pdf-page-113): survives and leads new nation |
| **Grax** | Hellfire leader, blames Grommel for Fraziel's death | [Prima p. 65](../prima/Goblin_Commander_Prima_2004_PAGE_INDEXED.md#pdf-page-066): dies in his gunpowder explosion |
| **Faine** | Stormbringer leader, seeks revenge for Fraziel | [Prima p. 81](../prima/Goblin_Commander_Prima_2004_PAGE_INDEXED.md#pdf-page-082): Grommel kills him after his lightning attack |
| **Syst** | Plaguespitter leader, whose anti-Grommel crusade is linked to Naxus | [Prima p. 97](../prima/Goblin_Commander_Prima_2004_PAGE_INDEXED.md#pdf-page-098): devoured by his pet carnivorous plant |
| **Naxus** | Nighthorde leader, murderer of Fraziel; uses creation Moonstone for soulless replicas | [Prima pp. 99–101](../prima/Goblin_Commander_Prima_2004_PAGE_INDEXED.md#pdf-page-102), [p. 112](../prima/Goblin_Commander_Prima_2004_PAGE_INDEXED.md#pdf-page-113): defeated, permanent death not established |
| **The Wise One / Old Man** | Plaguespitter shaman, one NPC identified by two titles; no personal name known | [Prima pp. 91–93](../prima/Goblin_Commander_Prima_2004_PAGE_INDEXED.md#pdf-page-094): collects 300 gold, falsely offers a truce and sends Grommel into a three-Slime ambush |

The original source for the commanders' creation order and the five named moons is the [Jaleco 2003 instruction manual, printed pp. 22–23](https://www.gamesdatabase.org/Media/SYSTEM/Microsoft_Xbox//Manual/formated/Goblin_Commander_-_Unleash_The_Horde_-_2003_-_Jaleco.pdf). The five commanders are “brothers” by shared creation, **not proved biological siblings with parents**.

## Moons (named places/celestial objects, NOT individual story characters)

| Commander | Clan | Moon | Moon color |
|---|---|---|---|
| Grommel | Stonekrusher | **Froxx** | white |
| Grax | Hellfire | **Heelinx** | red |
| Faine | Stormbringer | **Trist** | blue |
| Syst | Plaguespitter | **Phoust** | green |
| Naxus | Nighthorde | **Farthis** | purple |

Naming caution: purchasable **Nighthorde Moonstones** in [Prima p. 11](../prima/Goblin_Commander_Prima_2004_PAGE_INDEXED.md#pdf-page-012) are **black**, while the final Moongate for Nighthorde's color is **purple**. Neither discrepancy proves a sixth moon or an intentionally different religion.

## Unnamed but important individuals and groups

- The **non-hostile Plaguespitter messenger** who invites Grommel to meet the Wise One has no personal name in [Prima p. 91](../prima/Goblin_Commander_Prima_2004_PAGE_INDEXED.md#pdf-page-092).
- The **three rescued Stonekrusher workers** from [Rumble-Squish, Prima p. 49](../prima/Goblin_Commander_Prima_2004_PAGE_INDEXED.md#pdf-page-050) are unit roles, not individually named characters.
- **Nighthorde defectors** at a ruined Clanshrine join Grommel in [Prima p. 102](../prima/Goblin_Commander_Prima_2004_PAGE_INDEXED.md#pdf-page-103). This is actual described clan dissent, unlike the Wise One's uncorroborated claim.
- **Soulless replicas** made by Naxus using Fraziel's artifact are a category of enemies, not individually named characters. [Prima p. 101](../prima/Goblin_Commander_Prima_2004_PAGE_INDEXED.md#pdf-page-102).

**Not personal names:** Stone Ogre, Slime, Warpigpult, Battle Ball and Lightning Elemental are Titan/unit types. The five moons are not confirmed gods. Fanfiction characters (for example “Klop”) are non-canon.

## Two unreconciled character mysteries

**Fraziel's motives:** the [Prima guide calls Naxus's handling of the Great Machine a perversion (p. 99)](../prima/Goblin_Commander_Prima_2004_PAGE_INDEXED.md#pdf-page-100), yet [Naxus says the machine always produced soul bombs (p. 108)](../prima/Goblin_Commander_Prima_2004_PAGE_INDEXED.md#pdf-page-109). Neither establishes Fraziel's intended end state. See [Fraziel dossier](FRAZIEL_AND_ANCIENT_OGRISS.md).

**Naxus's fate:** the guide ends with his defeat and a peaceful unified goblin nation ([p. 112](../prima/Goblin_Commander_Prima_2004_PAGE_INDEXED.md#pdf-page-113)); permanent death or survival are not directly confirmed by those lines. Do not invent sequel canon.

## Source distinctions

The guide provides **official supplemental plot descriptions**, not a validated verbatim transcript of game dialogue. A named person count refers to distinct individuals/roles evidenced in the inspected materials; it is not a claim that there are no other unnamed NPCs or internal character IDs.
