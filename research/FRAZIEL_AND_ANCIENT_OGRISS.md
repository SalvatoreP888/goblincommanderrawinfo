# Fraziel, the ancient knowledge, and the missing humans of Ogriss
Research status: 2026-10-14 · Official manual / licensed Prima guide distinguished from secondary accounts.

## Short answer

**The game's surviving official lore does not identify Fraziel's human birthplace, teachers, how he acquired the Moonstone, where other humans lived, or who originally possessed the wisdom Nighthorde scavenged.** The story deliberately or incidentally leaves these histories unrecorded. We have specific evidence of ancient battlefields, crypts and forgotten knowledge, **not** a named ancient human empire.

This source is linked but **not stored in this repository**.
- **Prima guide:** [Stored original PDF](../Goblin_Commander_Unleash_The_Horde_Strategy_Guide_2004_Book.pdf); [complete indexed text](../prima/Goblin_Commander_Prima_2004_PAGE_INDEXED.md); SHA-256: `d97aa508cdc14fa0b89df6ee2009484ec6fffac304f4c79280755f9daa4690a7`.
- **Jaleco-era publisher story:** [LadyDragon reproduction](https://www.ladydragon.com/a-2goblin.html); calls Ogriss a goblin planet and Fraziel a **human wizard**, with commanders visiting him through spiritual projection.
- **Original official website program:** [Jaleco July 14, 2003 press release](https://www.nintendoworldreport.com/pr/8827/goblin-commander-website-open); planned monthly clan pages, not proof each was ultimately published.

## What Fraziel is actually known to have accomplished

The manual's goblin-perspective creation narrative attributes goblin **life, physical form, knowledge and purpose** to Fraziel using the power of the Moonstone. He created **five** moon-associated commanders and divided work among five clan societies to construct the Great Machine. This does **not** establish that Fraziel personally created their souls from nothing, created the Moonstone, or could create unlimited intelligent life without it. [Manual, printed pp. 22–23](https://www.gamesdatabase.org/Media/SYSTEM/Microsoft_Xbox//Manual/formated/Goblin_Commander_-_Unleash_The_Horde_-_2003_-_Jaleco.pdf).

The [Prima prologue, printed p. 43](../prima/Goblin_Commander_Prima_2004_PAGE_INDEXED.md#pdf-page-044) identifies Fraziel working alongside **Naxus** at the Great Machine. [p. 49](../prima/Goblin_Commander_Prima_2004_PAGE_INDEXED.md#pdf-page-050) describes the mine catastrophe and Fraziel's death. [p. 99](../prima/Goblin_Commander_Prima_2004_PAGE_INDEXED.md#pdf-page-100) says Naxus later admits murdering Fraziel. Fraziel's precise dying words have **not** been verified here with a timecoded retail-video transcript; “brother” is reported in secondary story summaries.

## Where did the ancient wisdom come from?

Naxus's **Nighthorde** is assigned to scour ancient cursed battlefields and crypts, recovering bones and forgotten wisdom. These locations supply cultural/historical depth, not an identified species or documented city. [Manual, printed pp. 22–23, 32–33](https://www.gamesdatabase.org/Media/SYSTEM/Microsoft_Xbox//Manual/formated/Goblin_Commander_-_Unleash_The_Horde_-_2003_-_Jaleco.pdf).

**Confirmed:** old battlegrounds, burial practices/tombs, recovered remains and “lost wisdom” are part of the Nighthorde setting. The clan's soul-related capabilities are documented in the [Prima Witch Doctor entry, p. 35](../prima/Goblin_Commander_Prima_2004_PAGE_INDEXED.md#pdf-page-036) and [Soul Vortex entry, p. 41](../prima/Goblin_Commander_Prima_2004_PAGE_INDEXED.md#pdf-page-042).

**Not confirmed:** human authorship of the wisdom, Fraziel personally receiving the finds, named ancient kingdoms, the ruins being from a known human culture, or original Moongate builders. The [2003 GameFAQs player-written walkthrough](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/ps2/589779-goblin-commander-unleash-the-horde/faqs/26986) uses additional phrases such as “ruined cities,” but this is **not independent publisher canon**.

**Temporal constraint:** Fraziel possessed creation magic before forming the Nighthorde. Their *later* scavenging alone cannot account for how he **originally learned** to make goblins. It does not rule out undocumented expeditions before their creation.

## Where are the other humans?

The manual's creation narrative references **humans collectively** and the master's own language, but only **Fraziel** is individually named as a human in the inspected story sources. No original document here identifies a human kingdom, another named human, homeland, temple, teacher or nearby settlement. [Manual, pp. 22–23](https://www.gamesdatabase.org/Media/SYSTEM/Microsoft_Xbox//Manual/formated/Goblin_Commander_-_Unleash_The_Horde_-_2003_-_Jaleco.pdf).

A contemporary Spanish magazine discusses humanity as a cultural contrast, but it is **journalistic narration**, not original geographic exposition. [Revista Oficial Xbox, issue 21](https://archive.org/details/Xbox_RO_21). The fan wiki's suggestion that Ogriss is separated from other races lacks a verified original citation. No source shows that other humans are dead, still living nearby, or from an entirely different planet. These are open hypotheses.

## Did Fraziel intend his Great Machine to destroy life?

The [Prima guide, p. 99](../prima/Goblin_Commander_Prima_2004_PAGE_INDEXED.md#pdf-page-100) calls Naxus's use of the machine a **perversion** of Fraziel's work, but [p. 108](../prima/Goblin_Commander_Prima_2004_PAGE_INDEXED.md#pdf-page-109) attributes to **Naxus** the revelation that the machine's purpose was **always** to manufacture soul-severing bombs. Naxus would kill Ogriss's inhabitants and use their souls to create a race in his own image.

**That does not prove Fraziel's personal intended use** of any hypothetical original bombs. It is incorrect either to assume Fraziel was innocent or to assert he planned extermination as settled canon. The conflict in descriptions remains a high-priority dialogue/script target.

A different but related ambiguity: [Prima p. 101](../prima/Goblin_Commander_Prima_2004_PAGE_INDEXED.md#pdf-page-102) identifies **Fraziel's Moonstone** as Naxus's source for **soulless goblin replicas**. [p. 11](../prima/Goblin_Commander_Prima_2004_PAGE_INDEXED.md#pdf-page-012) describes ordinary purchasable colored Moonstones as temporary clan-damage boosts. Neither source explains whether they are related artifacts or merely namesakes.

## Two kinds of ancient-world evidence, not a complete creation cosmology

- **Stone Ogre War** occurred before the main campaign: Stonekrushers subjugated the Stone Ogres in a struggle over the mines, proving other intelligent peoples existed alongside goblinkind. [Original manual, printed p. 24](https://www.gamesdatabase.org/Media/SYSTEM/Microsoft_Xbox//Manual/formated/Goblin_Commander_-_Unleash_The_Horde_-_2003_-_Jaleco.pdf).
- **Moongate network:** Master/Slave teleportation and a ritual timed to the blue moon Trist are documented in [Prima pp. 67–70](../prima/Goblin_Commander_Prima_2004_PAGE_INDEXED.md#pdf-page-070). No verified source credits any civilization or individual with inventing that network.

Neither establishes a human empire predating Fraziel, or that the Stone Ogres built ancient crypts.

## Official website recovery status

[Jaleco's 2003 website announcement](https://www.nintendoworldreport.com/pr/8827/goblin-commander-website-open) promised a monthly sequence of clan profiles beginning with Stonekrusher. A Nighthorde profile is a **plausible later entry**, but neither its actual publication nor any contents have been authenticated. The [Wayback original-domain lookup](https://web.archive.org/web/*/http://www.goblincommander.com/) is only a lead unless an actual capture can be retrieved and preserved.
